How this is counted
  • New logo = New Business pipeline, Closed Won, deal Amount (ARR). Ties to the TAM model's "New Biz Deals (HS)" tab deal for deal (1/1/2024 to 10/15/2026: 148 of 148, same amounts). One extra here: "Zayo Group - 2024 - SOW (Phase I POC)", $30K, which the model excludes as non-recurring.
  • Expansion = Expansion pipeline Upsell deals, plus pre-2025 mid-contract upsells booked in the Renewal pipeline (net recurring change). Mid-contract downgrades are excluded, so this is gross upsell. The Expansion pipeline starts in 2025.
  • Excluded: renewals, price-increase escalators, non-recurring-only and partner pipelines, deals with no close date, and closed-won deals dated in the future (pre-booked out-years of multi-year contracts).
  • State = the deal's or account's primary company HQ state. A multi-state operator lands entirely in its HQ state, not its service footprint.
  • Market = the 2027 Updated Sub-Vertical from the TAM model. Bookings companies use the model's classification; companies outside it are mapped from their HubSpot sub-vertical with the model's crosswalk.
  • Carve-outs (tagged) = Co-op, Engineering Firm, Government. International is its own view; TAM covers US and Canada only.
  • TAM accounts = all companies in the model. Customers = lifecycle Customer. Prospects = TAM less customers. SAM = prospects after the model's fiber and mapping screens. SOM = SAM after the fiber share, BDC, network-ownership and government-population screens. SOM $ = SOM accounts × the segment's average initial new-logo ARR (the model's method). Penetration = customers ÷ TAM accounts.
  • TAM is a snapshot of the model's "Updated TAM (CRM)" tab as of 2026-10-15; it does not refresh nightly. Totals tie to the model's TAM Summary (US: 12,264 TAM, 285 customers, 8,054 SAM, 5,743 SOM, $185.4M SOM $).
  • 2026 YTD runs through today. The YTD tile compares against 2025 through the same calendar date.
