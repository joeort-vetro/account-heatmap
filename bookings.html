How this is counted
  • New logo = New Business pipeline, Closed Won, deal Amount (ARR).
  • Expansion = Expansion pipeline Upsell deals, plus pre-2025 mid-contract upsells booked in the Renewal pipeline (net recurring change). Mid-contract downgrades are excluded, so this is gross upsell. The Expansion pipeline starts in 2025, so pre-2025 expansion depends on how those renewal deals were logged.
  • Excluded: renewals, price-increase escalators, non-recurring-only and partner pipelines, deals with no close date, and closed-won deals dated in the future (pre-booked out-years of multi-year contracts).
  • State = the deal's primary company HQ state in HubSpot. A multi-state operator's full deal lands in its HQ state, not its service footprint.
  • Market = the company's current HubSpot Sub-Vertical. Segments are being re-mapped to the new 2027 segment model; markets here will shift when that import lands.
  • Carve-outs (tagged) = Co-ops, Engineering Firms, Government / SLED. International is its own view.
  • 2026 YTD runs through today. The YTD tile compares against 2025 through the same calendar date.
